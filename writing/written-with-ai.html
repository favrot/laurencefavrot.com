With help from AI

My goals with this writing are to:

  1. Explicitly lay out my thinking for myself
  2. Build up enough context around these topics so I can effectively engage with people and LLMs

For that, speed and completeness matter more than every word originating from me.

My process is simple. I start by recording myself rambling on the topic. I then feed that transcript into an LLM, which helps me structure my ideas and surface related thinking that I missed. Structuring and editing is an iterative loop until I’m satisfied with the result. I’m accountable to every word on the page.

I want each piece to be enjoyable to read, to reflect my voice. But this writing is about extending what a person can do with AI. Refusing to let AI touch it would be incongruent with the entire thesis, so I make no apologies for the help.

If I had all the time in the world, I’d love to hand-carve every sentence. I don’t. I know that makes this writing a no-go for some people, and that’s their choice to make. Otherwise, I’d love to have you engage with these ideas.
