How the pieces fit together

You are the integrator of your own development. The writing and case studies here work out what that means, and how to build systems that help you do the integrating. The finished pieces are linked. The rest are sketches and will change.

The problem space

You as integrator sketch

Nobody can learn or grow for you. Specialists like teachers, doctors, and managers generally work inside their own domains, and nothing they hand you arrives fitted to your life. Integration is your job, whether you wanted it or not. Ask anyone who has had to navigate the medical system through a real illness.

Personal data sovereignty sketch

Integration runs on your data, which is now scattered across systems and providers. Prying these fragments back out is real work. Setting up and maintaining integrations is a technical challenge. You have to become part librarian and part data engineer.

Personal EHR sketch

One archive for every health record I have. The originals are preserved untouched forever, and everything I actually read is regenerated on top, so the archive improves as AI improves. There is almost no code, just files, procedures, and an agent to run them.

Surf advisor sketch

Given a door-to-door window, is surfing worth it, where should I go, and what should I bring? The evidence gets gathered deterministically and the judgment happens on top, fitted to my skill, my boards, and my access rather than to surfers in general. A surf day then credits the fitness advisor.
